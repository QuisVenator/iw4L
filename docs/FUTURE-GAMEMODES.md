Best Next Fits

- [ ] Oddball from Halo: one player carries a skull and earns points while holding it. Drop on death, maybe force a pass after 30 seconds. Low effort and uses the same carryable-objective logic as Kill Confirmed.
- [ ] Cranked from Call of Duty: a kill starts a short countdown; killing again resets it. The player gets faster while cranked and explodes when time expires. Mostly scoring, movement modifiers, warning sounds, and an explosion effect.
- [ ] One in the Chamber from Black Ops: one-shot pistol, one bullet, melee kills, limited lives. Very low effort because it is almost entirely weapon rules and scoring.
- [ ] Sharpshooter from Black Ops: everyone uses the same random weapon, which changes every 30–45 seconds. This could reuse most of Gun Game’s weapon progression machinery.
- [ ] Juggernaut: one player gets increased health, speed, and a distinctive model or color; the rest hunt them. Could score both for surviving as the juggernaut and for killing them.
- [ ] Team Defender from MW3: one team carries a flag while holding it, earning points over time. Existing flag/capture behavior should make this relatively cheap.
- [ ] Headhunter from Halo: players collect dropped skulls and deposit them in scoring zones. Custom skull asset, pickup logic, and deposit scoring.

Bigger But Plausible

- [ ] Payload / Escort from TF2 and Overwatch: a cart moves through checkpoints based on how many attackers are nearby. This needs a moving script model, waypoints, contest rules, and checkpoint sounds.
- [ ] Uplink / Gridiron from Advanced Warfare and Infinite Warfare: carry a drone into an enemy goal and dunk it. Could start with simple carrying rather than full throwing or passing.
- [ ] Infection from Halo: surviving players must last a timer while infected players convert them through melee. Good use of custom warning sounds and team changes.
- [ ] Horde / Firefight / Zombies: successive bot waves, increasing difficulty, and a currency system for buying doors and weapons. Higher effort, but much of it can build on the existing bots.
- [ ] Grifball: bomb carrier, goal zones, and melee combat. The scoring is straightforward; the main extra work is weapon and animation assets.
- [ ] Blitz from Ghosts: players capture a single zone near the enemy spawn. A simple inversion of Domination.
- [ ] Rush from Battlefield: attackers arm sequential objectives while defenders fall back. This could reuse existing bomb and zone logic.

Cheap Party Modes

- [ ] Sticks and Stones: crossbow, ballistic knife, and throwing knife; tomahawk kills subtract score. Very high fun-to-effort ratio.
- [ ] Instagib / Clan Arena: one-shot weapons or last-team-standing rounds with no respawns.
- [ ] Drop Zone or rotating Hardpoint: capture zones that move or appear periodically.
- [ ] Michael Myers / Hide and Seek: one armed hunter, everyone else with no weapon or limited movement.
- [x] Team Gun Game: the existing progression system, but with team scoring and team advancement.
